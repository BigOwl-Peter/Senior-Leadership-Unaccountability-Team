# Staff Survey

Once per career, a seeded HR survey arrives between simulation seconds 420 and 570 (35-47.5% through either session length). The survey has 120 simulation seconds for sign-off and a review 180 seconds after publication. Pausing freezes these deadlines. Legacy saves without a survey can receive it until second 850; completed careers are unchanged.

Results are an immutable snapshot of active, absent and notice-period staff. Each named response records team, office, morale, stress and a fictional engagement index: 60% morale plus 40% inverse stress. Index bands are engaged (70-100), not engaged (40-69) and actively disengaged (0-39). Teams are ranked on their mean individual index. These are game-specific thresholds, not Gallup scores or percentiles. No proprietary survey questions are used.

The player sees original results alongside the prospective board story. Reframing counts the middle group as positive. Selective reporting takes the highest-scoring half and exposes the changed denominator. Neither overwrites responses or reduces stress. Both improve board approval immediately while harming morale and trust, with a seeded chance of a later challenge. A challenge reduces approval and raises accountability and compliance risk.

The recovery option costs GBP 750 per respondent, improves live morale and stress, reduces team workload and builds trust. It keeps the original report unchanged. Unfiltered reporting is free and improves trust without funding operational relief. Missed sign-offs automatically publish the unfiltered report. Follow-up results reflect the current workforce, so staffing changes affect the comparison. Board approval, workforce health and trust feed existing scoring and operational systems.

Survey launch, publication and follow-up create leadership chat messages and notifications. A persistent launch banner opens the dedicated Staff Survey view. The Board retains the published headline and outcome. Save validation preserves the snapshot and enforces publication/review order. Restarting a career removes its survey.

Reference: [Gallup's engagement categories](https://www.gallup.com/394373/indicator-employee-engagement.aspx). This fictional assessment is not the Q12 instrument and is not validated or affiliated with Gallup.
